## Travel

The easiest way to get to campus is via the [Dartmouth Coach](https://dartmouthcoach.com/). It travels to/from campus from/to:

- Boston Logan Airport (any terminal)
- Boston South Station
- New York City: [150 E 42nd Street](https://dartmouthcoach.com/stop/new-york-city/)

There is also a train station in nearby White River Junction, and you can take the [Amtrak Vermonter](https://www.amtrak.com/routes/vermonter-train) to get here.

We may cover travel for you based on our budget, with priority given to presenters.

## Lodging

We have reserved space at the [Six South Street Hotel](https://sixsouth.com/), and we can organize a spot for you as you register for the conference!

Rooms are $289 a night; as with travel, we may cover this for you based on our budget, with priority given to presenters.
