<h1>Low Resource Computing</h1>
<aside>A workshop with talks, focused on <em>computing</em> with <em>constraints</em>.</aside>
<aside>Brought to you by <a title="Dartmouth Trust Lab" href="https://sites.dartmouth.edu/trustlab/">The Trust Lab</a>, the Cybersecurity Cluster, and ISTS.</aside>

## When
August 3 - August 6, 2025

## Where
Dartmouth College

## What
LRC is a chance to revisit computing with resource constraints. In an era with exascale machines and half a gigawatt data centers, and billion dollar clusters built with nanometer features, meaningful – essential – compututation remains practical with thousands of operations per second and nanowatt systems, and tens of cents, and hundreds of microns. This is an opportunity to rediscover the potential of everything from legacy silicon to the latest processing architecture. In between, we'll visit the realm of 'virtual' low-resource computing finding and fielding weird machines in unexpected places.
